⚙️ Burn Mechanism
1. Trading-fee Burn
The bonding curve burn mechanism applies to 1% of the XCX amount and 0.2% of the AI Influencer token amount in each eligible transaction. Monthly reports track XCX burns; AI Influencer token burns are accounted for separately.
2. Monthly Revenue-linked Burn
Each month, Xeleb applies 30% of reported revenue from bonding activities, marketplace fees, AI services, and other revenue streams to one of two methods:
Buyback & Burn: Use the allocated funds to repurchase XCX from the open market and permanently burn the acquired tokens.
Treasury Burn: Burn existing Treasury XCX with a reference value equivalent to 30% of reported monthly revenue.
Each monthly report identifies the method used. Treasury burn amounts are calculated as:
XCX burn amount = Reported monthly revenue × 30% ÷ Reference XCX price
3. Team Allocation Burn
The team may voluntarily burn a portion of its allocated $XCX, in addition to automatic burns and monthly revenue buybacks. Each burn will disclose the token amount, source wallet, and on-chain transaction for community verification.
